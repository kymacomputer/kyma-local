# v0.4.2

This release adds executable packs for Common Paper AI Addendum 1.0, CSA 3.0, DPA 1.1 and Bonterms AI Addendum 2.0.
Each pack produces a clause matrix and submits a source-bound assessment through the existing native tools.
The complete kit preserves the importer and revision behavior from v0.4.1.

The packs distinguish Input from its user-prompt subset and separate human authorship from Provider approval claims.
Typed fact checks reject strings and integers at boolean predicates.
Custom amendments, missing documents and unsupported choices require the current agent.

The release check exercises 44 hypothetical cases through OpenFeature and SQLite.
The examples remain unsigned, with all customer controls disabled.
Actual customer use, partner reaction and legal clearance remain separate outcomes.

See [the pack guide](../../standard-packs/README.md) for examples and the complete-contract route.
